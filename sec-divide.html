    Section 1.4 Dividing Fractions

    When dividing fractions, we first turn the problem into one of multiplication by taking the reciprocal of the second fraction;
    \begin{equation*} \frac{a}{c}\div\frac{b}{d}=\frac{a}{c}\times\frac{d}{b}. \end{equation*}
    Since the previous section looked at multiplication of fractions, this is now a problem we know how to solve.
    🔗

    Example 1.4.1. Dividing Fractions.

    Calculate \(\frac{5}{7}\div\frac{4}{9}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    We first convert the problem to one of multiplication;
    \begin{equation*} \frac{5}{7}\div\frac{4}{9}=\frac{5}{7}\times\frac{9}{4}=\frac{5\times 9}{7\times 4}=\frac{35}{36}. \end{equation*}
    This is in reduced form.
